Certificate manifest for "Two notions of tightness for the flat overhand knot"

- hashes-verify.txt : SHA-256 of verification/orchestration scripts, including archived source snapshots
- hashes-logs.txt   : SHA-256 of certificate logs and nested numerical-run logs
- hashes-artifacts.txt : SHA-256 of recorded JSON/JSONL data, SVG/PDF figures, and the manuscript PDF and bibliography
- The release archive contains the sources and printed manuscript from the same checkout.

Verify with:
  shasum -a 256 -c logs/hashes-verify.txt
  shasum -a 256 -c logs/hashes-logs.txt
  shasum -a 256 -c logs/hashes-artifacts.txt

The log files record outputs from the corresponding verification scripts.
Hashes establish file integrity, not mathematical validity.
The recorded 2026-10-23 figure-eight search evidence is under
verify/artifacts/n4-rerun-20261023/final/. Top-level sweep/walls/descent are
preserved interim runs; the top-level strata stores all geometry-independent
Newton solves, reclassified by final/strata_filter with the final predicate.
Each archived module is hashed against its original receipt. Current geometric
and numerical modules are also compared with the archived files. For n4_census,
n4_walls and n4_cells3 a Python syntax-tree comparison permits comment edits.
The current runner supports Git-free archives and fresh output directories,
and checks the input, lockfile and interpreter on resume.
The exact point/opening/chamber/outline certificates are separate evidence.
The trefoil pending-row log refreshes four rows only; other stored trefoil
census rows retain their earlier evidence. The figure-eight rerun does not
claim exhaustive roots or a full-cell local-optimality proof.

Earlier reachability, census-root and mutation logs are preserved in logs/history/.
